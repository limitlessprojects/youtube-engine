## Lessons

- **L-058 — Credentials need a durable mirror, not a secret dir.** The only
  copy of the PAT lived in `.secrets/` and died with the 4th recycle. Fix:
  every credential lives in `upload/` (the one dir recycles never touch) AND
  the working dir. `scripts/restore_workstation.sh` = one-command recovery.
  GitHub Secrets are write-only (runtime-only) — good for CI, useless for
  retrieval; the repo + upload/ are the real vaults.
- **L-059 — Autopsy your own frames with VLM before the audience does.**
  Pull `i.ytimg.com/vi/<id>/maxresdefault.jpg` (CDN, not bot-walled) and run
  the VLM audit as a standing gate step. It told us in 60 seconds what 195
  views took 3 days to confirm: 3.5/10 visual quality kills everything
  downstream.
- **L-060 — Outlier formulas beat topic envy.** The winning patterns in our
  niche: belief correction ("X doesn't work the way you think"), everyday
  curiosity (hidden mechanism in plain sight), superlatives ("dumbest/worst/
  fewest"). AI-fear prints 7.9x for Kurzgesagt but needs 8/10 execution we
  don't have yet. Match formula to execution ceiling.
- **L-061 — The LLM-free CI heartbeat carried the studio through its agent's
  death.** Deterministic loop (Actions) + creative sessions (agent) is the
  right architecture: transcription/blueprints/radar never stopped, queue kept
  popping, ledger kept committing. Single-point-of-failure was the agent's
  sandbox, and L-058 closes that gap.
