## What worked

- **L-062 — The art-director autopsy loop scales.** Scoring contact sheets
  (not single frames) found systemic faults (flat void composition, floating
  elements, camouflaged birds) that per-frame checklists can't see; per-frame
  gates then verified the fixes. Two VLM layers > one.
- **L-063 — Contrast is a two-variable equation.** Dark silhouettes vanished
  on the dark stage panels (bird #10182E ≈ panel #101832) — the fix wasn't
  "bigger birds" but LIT birds (cream bodies) on dark panels, dark birds on
  lit sky. One palette rule: subjects are the opposite value of their ground.
- **L-064 — Verify the verifier.** The gate's high-severity "caption overlaps
  character legs at y~1650" claims were the CAPTION WORDS THEMSELVES — same
  cream #F6EFE3 as the hero's skin. A 20-line pixel probe refuted 3 high
  flags in one minute. Every spatial claim gets pixels or it didn't happen.
- **L-065 — Gate the beat, not just the scene.** Uniform keyframes (u25/60/90)
  never sampled the 1-second stamp beats, so the gate flagged "missing assets"
  that were simply in unsampled beats. Beat-timed probe frames (word-timed,
  like everything else) fixed the false-fail class; s5 went 3×FIX → 3×PASS.
- **L-066 — Control tests settle TTS disputes.** "birds" heard as singular
  6/6 across two synths — a normal-rate control proved the ASR model drops
  the plural, not edge-tts. Punchline re-cut at +30% anyway (it's the joke),
  bias whitelisted in the report with the evidence attached.
- Synthesized sound design (numpy pad/riser/whoosh/thump) = license-free at
  $0, no download lottery — v3-spec #2 closed.

## What broke

  batch and half-applied the rest — edits now verified by grep before moving on.

  WAV + sleeps 2s between lines.

- Heavy hook scenes (236 birds/frame) render ~3.6s/frame — 1000-frame render
  ≈ 60 min. Acceptable for tonight; a bird-sprite cache is the v4 fix.

## Lessons

- **L-062 — Autopsy the sheet, then gate the frame.** Contact-sheet scoring
  finds systemic craft gaps; per-frame checklists verify fixes. Run both.
- **L-063 — Subjects are the opposite value of their ground.** Check subject
  vs background LAYOUT value before any detail work; camo kills diagrams.
- **L-064 — Pixel-probe every spatial gate claim.** Same-colored UI and
  characters produce confident false collisions. Pixels or it didn't happen.
- **L-065 — Beat-timed probe frames for fast-cut scenes.** If cuts are ~1s,
  u25/60/90 sampling is blind; sample at the beats (word timing, always).
- **L-066 — Control-test the verifier before re-cutting the audio.** A
  normal-rate control cost 30 seconds and saved the punchline from a
  phantom defect — and documented a real ASR bias for the whole pipeline.
