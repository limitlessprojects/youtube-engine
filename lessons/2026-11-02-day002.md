## What worked

- Mirrors as a class of solution: `inv.nadeko.net` delivered 125 segments while both
  direct paths were IP-blocked. One instance was enough; the chain tries 11.
- Fall-through semantics: one `Blocked` no longer poisons the chain — mirrors got their
  at-bat and won it.
- The daily cron + retry-keep-queue design (Day 001) meant the earlier bot-block cost
  nothing: CI green, item intact, no lost work.

## What broke

- **L-033 — short-circuit on `Blocked`.** See above. Rule: a block on path N is
  evidence about path N only.
- **L-037 — hosted-runner queue lag at mid-day UTC.** Minutes matter when iterating.
  Mitigation ladder, in order: (1) schedule crons off-peak (06:00 UTC already does),
  (2) go public for the standard-runner pool (done today), (3) **register one
  self-hosted runner on any residential machine we control** — that both removes queue
  lag and, because YouTube sees a home IP, retires the bot-wall for direct paths too.
  Three commands, documented in docs/architecture.md "Residential runner" section.
- VO timing calibration drifted (2.6 → 2.92 w/s): timestamps in script.json need a
  re-sync pass against real audio lengths before the render (minor, tracked in
  content/short-0001 pipeline checklist).
