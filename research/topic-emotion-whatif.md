# Emotional / trending What-If topics — candidate pool (2026-11-14, boss-requested)

Boss asked for science topics with emotion + trend/contention voltage. Truth first, then the pool.
**Rules for this lane (get them before any script freeze):**
1. **Emotion is not permission to fabricate.** Every claim must point at a peer-reviewed or documented source. No talisman morals, no mythic certainty, no winner-narrative.
2. **No invented grievance of a real identifiable group.** Politics, war, religion: if we can't ground it in a public, contemporaneously-verified fact pattern, we don't use it.
3. **Contention you can stand behind > contention that's just loud** — mine friction physics/physiology/healthcare-systems, not body-count numbers or.position-free tragedy.
4. **Emotion attaches to a process (love, fear, loss, hope) — never to a person we can hurt.**
5. Hard Nope: weapon capability / victim role-play / invasion scenarios / threats to children presented as spectacle.

## What "emotion" translates to in our engine

- L-085 "silhouette count ≠ depth; one subject per frame" still rules — emotional shot ~ #0003 f01: one figure, one environment.
- Video notes (CRATE corpus): Zack anatomy fear/awe lane = spectacle-first no-text hook; Kurzgesagt prompts = voice-over-dominant over photoreal background; we adopt BOTH — e.g. opening on a heartbeat only (audio-led black frame) so L-073 can orient without text.
- **Reaction face slot (S3 when we hit emotional payoff):** no eyebrow-sneer; chase the specific emotion with unmistakable pose (relief = shoulders drop; fear = no body motion; awe = complete stillness) — never the stock "comic surprise" face our corpus warns against.
- **Staging:** emotion comes from the environment quietness BEFORE the cut, not the display. Black bird fall-30-second loops are out; short before-cut breath is in.
