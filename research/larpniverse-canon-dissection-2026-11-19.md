> In this town, everybody says "hush hush hush." You see, I made a video about the while

## 4. The measured formula (what the four canon texts share)

**The 10-beat canon skeleton (present in A/B/C; D runs the horror variant):**
1. **Opener = the rule.** "In this town, everybody ___ except me." (or "everybody says
   X" — A's meta variant). The title IS the opener. 7–9 words.
2. **Taxonomy gag.** Three absurd specifics, deadpan ladder: pool/pizza/goldfish (B);
   birthday/a-Tuesday/nice-rock (C). One of the three is always a recursive absurdity
   (a party FOR the mayor's goldfish; a party BECAUSE someone found a rock).
3. **Named recurring cast.** Kevin (rival/friend), Linda (neighbor), grandma (absurd
   competence: push-ups, Tuesday dancing), the mayor (fraud authority — "He has never
   fixed a single pothole"), the mayor's goldfish (mascot). **The universe is serial** —
   the same cast recurs across videos; that's the retention flywheel (viewers follow
   characters, not one-shots).
4. **Discipline montage with mantra + escalating precision.** C's "Fold, fold, tuck,
   roll. Four seconds: too slow → Two seconds. One second." The mantra is the
   quotable/memeable atom. ALWAYS with a measurable number that improves.
5. **The chant (HUSS).** Crowd sound, not narration — the VO never chants the hype;
   the town does. Deployed at montage peaks and the closer.
6. **The test stage.** "The biggest party of the year" — the canon's recurring
   apocalypse-calendar. The discipline pays off HERE, publicly.
7. **The silence beat.** "The music stopped. The whole crowd went silent." (B) /
   "No music, no party lights, nothing." (B closer half) — the pre-reveal held breath.
   The genre ALWAYS goes quiet before the turn. (Our #0011 HOLD rig is this exact beat.)
8. **Recognition + the credit line.** "While you were out partying, by the way." —
   the meme's signature phrase, delivered deadpan as the answer to "how did you do
   that?" The credit line is the video's thesis in one sentence.
9. **Inversion/twist.** B: the town copies the grind → now I'm the one free on Friday
   (status inversion, wholesome). C: didn't get the job → "I had already started" (the
   competence twist). A: they copied my new sound → "I'm already making the next one"
   (the originator twist). D: rescue → the sting ("But who's going to free me?").
   Twist is 4–8 words, immediately followed by...
10. **Hard cut, no CTA.** Zero of the canon ends on a question-to-viewer or
    follow-beg. The chant closer (B/C) or the sting (D) or the thesis line (A) is the
    last sound. (ENDING_RULE v2 confirmed against the source canon.)

**Writing micro-rules (measured):** ~15–20% of sentences are 1–5-word kickers
("Nothing." / "The music stopped." / "The cat is fine. The cat is warm." / "I only
wrap."). Reassurance-after-absurdity is a signature C move ("The cat is fine. The cat
is warm."). Numbers written as words in VO, digits allowed on screen (our L-090 was
right). 0 exclamation marks in narration — ALL caps energy lives in the visuals
(HUSS! stamps) and the crowd bed, never the narrator.

**Visual system (VLM-measured across the canon):** Roblox-style blocky machinima,
no HUD, cinematic third-person; word-by-word karaoke captions (SINGLE words pop in
sequence — VLM read consecutive single words "a / they / dancing / HUSS! / party"
across 6.25s-spaced frames on B); HUSS! gets oversized stylized treatment. Palette
arcs: night-blue town → saturated party warm → dark again (B), cool purple night →
candy pink interior → dark (D). Day/night bookends. Frame-rate/res mix across the
folder (30/60fps, 576–1080p) — the genre tolerates production variance; the WRITING
and the chant carry it. Branding: the hoodie "in this town! beyond the rules" is
@larpniverse's merch/sign-off — **their IP; we do not touch it.** Kevin/Linda/grandma/
mayor as generic recurring cast is genre convention (fair game), but our vindictive
note: copying their cast verbatim makes us the Kevin of video A. Build OUR cast.
