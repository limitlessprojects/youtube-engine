## 03 Craft notes
- Light: practical/emissive only — sodium/Xenon pad lights on the vehicle, then ignition. Extreme dynamic range: near-black sky vs 10+ stop emissive plume.
- Contrast: brutal; blacks are crushed to true black (night), highlights clipped by design; lens flare and bloom are features.
- Motion register: violent vertical ascent, camera mostly static or slow tracking pan; steam expansion is the secondary motion.
- Subject discipline: one subject (vehicle) + byproducts (steam, flame, smoke); no people in frame; composition centers the vehicle with generous black negative space.
