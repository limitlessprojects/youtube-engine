## What the transcripts teach (10 second digest)

1. **Every hook is a promise of the title, restated aloud in second person in the first ~15 words** — Zack: "when you get stitches…" → title is "How Stitches Work". Kurzgesagt: "you were on your way home when you died" (The Egg). MinutePhysics: "What if you try to sandwich a cube between two portals?" (title identical). **Title ≈ spoken first line** — our topics should pick title+first-15-words as the SAME sentence.
2. **Belief-correction opener for Short science** (MinutePhysics): "You probably learned that…" then the counter. Strong candidate for #0006's cold open.
3. **Zack SFX-driven explainers:** 0 text upfront, one SFX-timed reveal; his corpus never uses stamp cards — graphics carry the story.
4. **MinuteEarth subscribe-at-tag-in** — none of our 18 corpus items uses a "like-and-subscribe" CTA within first 30 words; none of them hurts conversion. Gating holds (no CTAs before the value lands).
5. **Question/FAQ cadence** — nearly every corpus script opens with a question (or an event) — hook-as-question is the dominant grammar.
