## Fact sheet — verified 2026-11-07 (DO NOT weaken or inflate)

- **"About seven neighbors"**: Ballerini et al. 2008, PNAS — starlings interact
  with a fixed NUMBER of nearest neighbors, six or seven on average
  (topological interaction). Phrase as "about seven", never "exactly".
- **Veer right + altitude split**: Schiffner & Srinivasan 2016 (budgerigar
  flight-tunnel studies, QBI Queensland) — two collision-avoidance strategies:
  (a) veer to the right, (b) pass above/below.
- **CUT CLAIM (do not restore)**: "bird reaction time 10x humans" — FALSE.
  Crow data (Oct 2026): ~235ms visual decisions vs human ~250ms — barely
  faster, and not a flocking fact anyway.
- **Softened claim**: no "ripples in under a second" — not verified; the
  approved line is "the whole murmuration bends with it".
