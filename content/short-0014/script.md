## Fact sources (S7 — every checkable claim)

- Absorbable sutures are left in place and degrade in tissue: Ethicon *Wound Closure
  Manual* (2005) §"Absorbable Materials"; Chu & von Fraunhofer, *Wound Closure
  Biomaterials* (1997).
- Synthetic absorbables (polyglycolide, polyglactin 910, poliglecaprone 25) are
  polyesters degraded by **hydrolysis** — water cleaves the ester bonds: Ethicon WCM;
  (for the schema: one entry per beat below).
- Polyglactin 910 retains ~50% tensile strength at 2 weeks (Ethicon VICRYL spec
  sheet); poliglecaprone 25 loses ~50% within 1 week.
- Fragment phagocytosis by macrophages as part of the foreign-body response: Ethicon
  WCM tissue response section.
- Tuned absorption: fast-absorbing materials for skin/mucosa (fast-absorbing gut),
  months-long material for fascia (PDS II ~ minimal until 6 months per Ethicon data).
- Non-absorbable materials (polypropylene) are effectively permanent: Ethicon WCM.
